Gray Zone Warfare Factions and PMCs

Who you are on Lamang Island, how faction vendors work, and why other contractors are as dangerous as AI on the same roads.

PMCs in a gray zone

Gray Zone Warfare is built around private military companies operating in a conflict that is not a clean war. You are a PMC: hired, equipped, and sent onto Lamang Island to complete work. Other players are rival contractors. AI fills checkpoints, compounds, and jungle paths.

If you are new, start with the beginner guide 2026. Combat ranges are in the weapons guide.

Factions, vendors, and tasks

Factions are your employers and shops. Reputation and completed contracts unlock gear. That is why quest routing matters as much as PvP. Selling loot to those vendors is the economy loop.

PMC vs bot tracking is explained on the ESP page for players comparing awareness tools.

Reading other contractors

  • Gunfire in a town often means a PMC fight, not only AI
  • Extract pads concentrate rival teams — extraction guide
  • Sound discipline still applies — tips and tricks

Factions FAQ

What is a PMC in Gray Zone Warfare?

A private military contractor. You, other players, and hired companies share Lamang Island with AI forces.

Do factions change how I play?

They change vendors and contracts. The island is still shared PvPvE.

Are other players always hostile?

Treat unknowns as threats, especially at extracts.
