Gray Zone Warfare Map Guide

How to read Lamang Island — terrain, loot areas, extract zones, and rotate paths that keep you alive through a full GZW raid.

Lamang Island overview

Lamang Island is the main Gray Zone Warfare play space — a mix of dense jungle, compounds, roads, and open fields. Fights often happen at 200–600 meters on exposed ground, while close-quarters clears happen inside buildings and tree lines.

New players should start with the beginner guide before learning advanced rotates here.

Terrain and sightlines

Jungle & cover

  • Dense foliage breaks line of sight — ambushes are common on trails
  • Elevation gives overwatch advantage on valleys and roads
  • Compound clears are loud; expect PMCs to investigate

Open ground

  • Cross open areas quickly or not at all without squad cover
  • Long-range duels favor stable rifles — see weapons guide

Extract zones and routing

Helicopter extract pads are your exit. Pick an exfil at raid start, then route loot runs toward it — not away. Players searching for map-level awareness often read our Gray Zone Warfare Radar page, which explains how extract markers and loot ESP cover radar-style routing.

Map FAQ

What map does Gray Zone Warfare use?

Lamang Island — jungle, towns, military sites, and coastal extracts for PMC raids.

Where are extracts on Lamang Island?

Helicopter pads around the island. Learn locations and rotate early before exfils get crowded.

How do I navigate dense jungle?

Use landmarks and elevation. Move slower, listen for gunfire, and avoid blind trail pushes.
