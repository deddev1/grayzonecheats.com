Gray Zone Warfare Extraction Guide

Helicopter exfils on Lamang Island decide whether a raid counts. This guide covers when to leave, how pads get camped, and how to plan a second exit.

How extraction works

In Gray Zone Warfare you keep what you extract with. Dying or missing the helicopter means that kit stays on the island. Extracts are typically helicopter pads placed around Lamang Island. Call the extract, hold the area, and board when the bird arrives.

Island layout is in the Lamang Island guide and map guide. New players should read the beginner guide first.

Extract habits that save kits

  • Pick an exit at spawn — loot toward it, not away from it
  • Leave with “enough” — a half bag extracted beats a full bag lost
  • Watch the pad from cover — approach last, not first, if you hear a fight
  • Name a fallback pad — one camped extract should not end the raid

Sound and rotate discipline is also in tips and tricks. For extract marker tools, see radar-style awareness and features.

Why extracts get contested

Late raid, everyone with loot converges on the same pads. AI still patrols nearby roads. That mix is why helicopter campers exist. Bring ammo for a short hold, not a 10-minute siege. If you are questing, finish the objective before you commit to a loud pad — see quests and contracts.

Extraction FAQ

How do you extract in Gray Zone Warfare?

Reach a helicopter extract on Lamang Island and complete the exfil before time runs out. Death or a missed bird drops your raid loot.

When should I start rotating?

As soon as the bag has value you cannot replace. Late crowded pads wipe more raids than empty jungles.

Are extracts always safe?

No. Have cover, listen, and keep a fallback pad.
