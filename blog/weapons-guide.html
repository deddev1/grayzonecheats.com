Gray Zone Warfare Weapons Guide

Gun roles, recoil habits, and range bands for Lamang Island — how to pick loadouts that match GZW's open-world firefights.

Understanding GZW gunfights

Gray Zone Warfare weapons behave like realistic military sims — recoil, ballistics, and optics matter. Most PMC fights on Lamang Island happen at medium to long range on hills and roads, with sudden close fights in compounds.

Start with the beginner guide if you are new to extractions.

Assault rifles

Best all-rounders for mixed terrain. Mod for stability first; add magnification for open valleys on the map.

DMRs & battle rifles

Strong at 200–600 m if you control recoil between shots. Pair with good positioning intel from ESP.

SMGs & CQB

Situational for compound clears. Risky on open rotates where snipers and DMRs dominate sightlines.

Recoil and combat tools

Controlled bursts win medium-range fights. Players who need help landing follow-up shots often review the aimbot guide and features guide for FOV-limited targeting and no recoil tuning — always use conservative settings to avoid obvious play.

Weapons FAQ

Best weapon for beginners?

Stable 5.56 rifles with controllable recoil. Learn range bands before chasing meta loadouts.

Most common engagement range?

200–600 m on open ground; CQB inside buildings and jungle trails.

How important is recoil control?

Critical at medium range. See aimbot page for package combat tools.
