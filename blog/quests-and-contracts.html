Gray Zone Warfare Quests and Contracts

How PMC tasks work on Lamang Island — chain objectives on a short route, extract the turn-in, and unlock vendors without wasting kits.

What contracts are

Gray Zone Warfare progression is not only PvP. Factions hand out contracts: recover a document, visit a compound, eliminate AI at a site, or deliver an item. Completing them opens traders, better kits, and the next task chain. Read factions and PMCs for who you work for.

Location names make more sense after the Lamang Island guide.

Routing quests like a loot run

  • Stack tasks in one region — three nearby pickups beat one across the map
  • Extract the objective — quest items die with you; see the extraction guide
  • Bring a cheap kit for first visits — scout the building before you bring a meta rifle
  • Loot on the same path — combine with loot and economy

Key and quest highlight tools are described on the features page and in the features guide.

Contracts FAQ

What are contracts in Gray Zone Warfare?

Faction tasks: recover items, clear sites, or visit locations. Rewards unlock vendors and further work.

Should I quest and loot in the same raid?

Yes if the objective is on your extract path. Skip island-wide detours on expensive kits.

Do quest items extract with me?

They must survive the raid. Plan the pad first.
