Gray Zone Warfare Loot and Economy

What to pick up on Lamang Island, what to sell to vendors, and when a backpack is valuable enough to extract instead of hunting one more crate.

Raid loot vs stash value

Every Gray Zone Warfare raid is a bet: the kit you bring versus the items you extract. Vendors buy what you survive with. That loop funds the next rifle — see the weapons guide before you spend a wipe’s savings on one gun.

Containers and compounds are mapped in the map guide and Lamang Island guide.

What is usually worth the slot

  • Quest items and keys — irreplaceable for contracts
  • Meds and ammo you use — weight that prevents a death is profit
  • High value, small size — electronics, rare parts, compact valuables
  • Guns you will actually run — a third rifle you cannot equip is dead weight

Leave when the bag is “good enough.” Timing is in the extraction guide. Container visibility is covered on ESP and in ESP explained.

Wipes and prices

Patches and wipes can change what vendors pay. Before you farm a route for a week, check the updates guide and live product updates. Habits that keep raids short are in tips and tricks.

Loot FAQ

What loot is worth taking?

High value-to-weight items, usable ammo and meds, keys, and quest items. Dump bulky junk if it slows the extract.

How does the economy work?

Sell extracted items to faction vendors and buy kits. Wipes can change prices.

Should I fight over loot?

Only if the bag is worth the kit. Third parties punish long fights.
