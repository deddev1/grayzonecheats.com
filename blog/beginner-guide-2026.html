Gray Zone Warfare Beginner Guide 2026

Everything a new PMC needs before the first Lamang Island deployment — how raids work, what to loot, when to extract, and where to learn more.

What is Gray Zone Warfare?

Gray Zone Warfare is a tactical open-world extraction shooter from MADFINGER Games. You deploy to Lamang Island as a private military contractor, loot gear, complete contracts, fight AI and other players, and extract before time runs out. Unlike arena shooters, survival and information matter more than kill count.

Read the map guide for terrain basics and the weapons guide for gun selection after your first few raids.

First-session checklist

  • Learn one region — pick a small area instead of crossing all of Lamang Island
  • Plan an extract early — know your exit before you loot (see map guide)
  • Bring meds and ammo — mid-range rifles suit most beginner fights
  • Avoid prolonged fights — third parties are common on open trails
  • Extract with something — a small successful raid beats a full-kit death

Loot, contracts, and progression

Contracts and loot drive progression. Search buildings methodically, mark quest objectives on your map, and rotate toward extract when your inventory has value. Players who want faster routing often read our ESP explained article and features guide to understand awareness tools.

For advanced raid habits, continue to tips and tricks. Track patches in the updates guide.

Beginner FAQ

Is Gray Zone Warfare hard for beginners?

GZW has a steep learning curve because of long sightlines, mixed PvP/PvE pressure, and timed extractions. Start with short loot routes and prioritize extracting over chasing kills.

What should I bring on my first raid?

A reliable mid-range rifle, basic meds, and a planned extract. Avoid expensive kits until you know Lamang Island flow.

How do extractions work?

Reach a designated extract — often helicopter pads — before the raid timer ends. Plan your route early; crowded exfils are dangerous.
