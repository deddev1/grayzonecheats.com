Gray Zone Warfare Tips and Tricks

Practical Lamang Island habits — routing, extracts, sound discipline, and tool usage that improve survival without flashy play.

Before you deploy

  • Pick one loot route from the map guide — do not improvise every raid
  • Choose an extract at raid start; work backward from the helicopter pad
  • Check updates after patches
  • Match your rifle to expected range — weapons guide

During the raid

Awareness

  • Pause at trail mouths — jungle muffles sound but not gunfire
  • Use ESP to spot PMCs before crossing open ground
  • Route with extract & loot intel instead of random building clears

Combat

  • Fight only when the loot or position justifies it
  • Hold angles at 200–600 m with stable rifles
  • Save combat tools for emergencies — not every engagement

Extract smarter

Leave when you are ahead, not when the timer panics you. Early extracts avoid last-minute PMC stacks at helicopter pads. If you are new, pair this with the beginner guide and features guide to understand which tools help routing vs combat.

Tips FAQ

Best tip for new players?

Extract with profit early. Short wins teach the island faster than full-kit deaths.

When should I extract?

When inventory has value or nearby gunfire signals danger — not at the last second.

Using tools safely?

Conservative settings, enable only what you need. Read EAC FAQ.
