Top questions before you buy

Does this work on the current Gray Zone Warfare build?

Compatibility is maintained for the current Gray Zone Warfare build on Windows PC. Check the Updates page before each session — especially after game patches. Gray Zone Warfare uses Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC), and we do not guarantee undetected status. Avoid running outdated builds after major updates.

What do I need to run it?

You need a Windows 10 or Windows 11 PC (64-bit), a valid Gray Zone Warfare Steam account, and an active license ($35 monthly or $150 lifetime). After purchase, follow the digital delivery instructions to install the loader. The Cloud-DMA option requires compatible DMA hardware if you choose that deployment path — confirm requirements on the Features page and Support page before buying.

Is the overlay stream-proof?

Yes. A stream-proof overlay is included in the package. It uses a capture-safe mode designed to stay hidden from common recording and streaming tools, so overlays remain visible to you without appearing on recordings. See the stream-proof demo on the homepage gallery.

Monthly vs lifetime — what is the difference?

Both plans include the same full feature set: PMC and bot ESP, loot container ESP, extract helicopter markers, key and quest highlights, aimbot with FOV limit, no recoil tuning, stream-proof overlay, and Cloud-DMA option. Monthly is $35 USD for 31 days of access. Lifetime is $150 USD for unlimited-duration access. Compare plans at checkout or on the Pricing page.

How do I get support after purchase?

Visit the Support page for installation help, license activation, and troubleshooting. Have your order confirmation or license details ready when you contact support through the purchase support channel. For patch-related issues, also check the Updates page.
