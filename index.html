What is Gray Zone Warfare?

Gray Zone Warfare (GZW) is a tactical extraction shooter for Windows PC on Steam, made by MADFINGER Games. You play as a private military contractor (PMC) on Lamang Island — a large open map with jungle, towns, and long-range firefights.

What happens in a raid

  • Drop in — spawn on the island with your loadout
  • Loot & complete contracts — find gear in buildings and containers, finish mission objectives
  • Fight AI and other players — you face bots and real PMCs in the same raid
  • Extract in time — reach a helicopter exfil before the raid ends, or you lose what you brought in

Information wins most raids: who is nearby, where the good loot is, and which extract is safest. Dense jungle and open hills make that harder than in a standard shooter.
